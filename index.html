About Me

👋 Hey there! I’m Stelios, a passionate web developer crafting digital experiences right here in Thessaloniki, Greece. I thrive on turning ideas into visually stunning and highly functional websites. Think of me as someone who sweats the details — from clean and semantic HTML and stylish CSS, to dynamic JavaScript interactions and robust backend logic with PHP and MySQL. With solid experience in WordPress development and Elementor customization, I love creating user-friendly, responsive websites that clients can easily manage and update. I’ve also honed my skills in OpenCart development, working extensively with the Journal theme and customizing plugins to meet unique business needs, ensuring seamless functionality and tailored e-commerce solutions. Whether I’m building sleek user interfaces with ReactJS or structuring complex applications with Angular, I’m always eager to dive into the latest technologies and level up my skills. For me, web development isn’t just a job – it’s a continuous journey of learning and creating. When I’m not immersed in code, you’ll likely find me unwinding with some video games 💻, strategizing at the billiards table 🎱, or enjoying quality time with my family. Let’s connect and build something amazing together!
